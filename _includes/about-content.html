I am a Doctor of Engineering (D.Eng.) student at the Singapore Institute of Technology (SIT), conducting research under the supervision of Prof. Mahesh Raveendranatha Panicker at the Ultrasound Imaging Lab and the SIT x NVIDIA AI Centre. My research focuses on the intersection of deep learning and computational medical imaging, specifically developing specialized deep learning frameworks to optimize ultrasound image reconstruction. I hold a Bachelor of Technology in Electronics and Communication Engineering with a minor in Machine Learning from APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University (KTU). Driven by the potential of hardware-accelerated and physics-informed AI, my goal is to bridge advanced signal processing techniques with modern deep learning paradigms to enhance diagnostic imaging fidelity.
