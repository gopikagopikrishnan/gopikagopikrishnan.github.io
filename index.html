|ABOUT ME

Teaching Ultrasound Systems to adapt.

I'm Gopika, a Doctor of Engineering Scholar at the Singapore Institute of Technology, working with Prof. Mahesh Raveendranatha Panicker in the Pulse Echo Lab. I build deep learning beamformers that stay reliable on new probes, new tissue and new tasks.

|RESEARCH

Resilient Deep Beamforming

ISBI 2026

ADAPT

A depth-agnostic, patch-wise multibeamformer that fuses DAS, FDMAS and Capon experts in parameter space: interpretable and data-efficient.

Preprint

PIVOT

Intent-conditioned single plane-wave beamforming: one network switches between resolution and contrast regimes on request.

Ongoing

RESONATE

Region-adaptive neural apodization with time-of-flight experts for resolution, contrast and speckle, blended by a learned gate.

Ongoing

Continual learning

For life-long learning and adaptability of different on-board deep beamformers.
